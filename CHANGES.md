- Cockpit theme: small **animations**. Switches slide instead of jumping, buttons change colour gradually under the mouse, windows fade in when they open, and the light of CPU Tuner / Free VRAM breathes while it is working with DCS. A click never waits for an animation. The other themes are unchanged (`glide`, `mix_colour`, `Switch._pos`, `RoundButton._mix` / `_fade`, `Revealable._fade_in`, `NovaLauncher._pulse_leds`, theme style `motion` / `MOTION`).
